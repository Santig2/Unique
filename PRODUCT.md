# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Clients in Austin, TX looking for premium catering for weddings, corporate galas, and events, as well as high-end custom bakery goods.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Chef Patricia's culinary artistry, build trust through testimonials and visuals, and drive quotes/consultations for events.

## Brand Personality
Elegant, artisanal, warm, premium.

## Anti-references
Cheap fast-food styles, chaotic layouts, overly corporate/bland SaaS templates.

## Design Principles
- Visual Elegance: Let the food photography and typography speak for themselves.
- Artisanal Warmth: Use delicate textures (sourdough, cream) and signature pink touches.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high contrast on text, especially over images or pink backgrounds. Ensure interactive elements are large enough for touch targets.
